FAQ

Before you start

How long does a website take?

It depends on size: a simple site usually takes a few days, larger ones a few weeks. After we talk, I'll give you an exact timeline.

What do I need to prepare?

Your business name, logo (if you have one), texts and photos. If you don't have them ready, I'll help you organise them.

Can I change things after launch?

Yes. You can request changes any time, or choose the monthly support plan.

Which languages can the site be in?

Albanian, English, German or others. The RR-NetzMontage site and this portfolio are multilingual examples.
